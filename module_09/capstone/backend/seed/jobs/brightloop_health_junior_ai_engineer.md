Title: Junior AI Engineer
Company: Brightloop Health
Location: Remote (US)

Brightloop Health builds scheduling and intake software for community clinics. Our AI team is small: three engineers and a product lead. This role works on the assistant that answers front-desk staff questions from clinic policy documents.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain retrieval pipelines that ground answers in clinic policy documents
- Write FastAPI endpoints that the web app and the staff assistant call
- Run evaluation sets every week and track answer accuracy and citation rates
- Containerize services with Docker and keep them running in our staging cluster
- Pair with senior engineers on code review and incident follow-ups

## Requirements

- 1+ year of professional or project experience with Python
- Experience building REST APIs, ideally with FastAPI or Flask
- Hands-on work with a vector database such as ChromaDB, pgvector or Pinecone
- Understanding of retrieval-augmented generation: chunking, embeddings, prompt design
- Comfortable with SQL and relational databases (we use PostgreSQL)
- Git and pull-request based workflow

## Nice to have

- Experience evaluating LLM output (RAGAS, custom eval sets, LLM-as-judge)
- Familiarity with HIPAA or handling sensitive health data
- Docker Compose for local development

## Pay and benefits

$95,000 to $115,000, full health coverage, a $1,500 yearly learning budget.

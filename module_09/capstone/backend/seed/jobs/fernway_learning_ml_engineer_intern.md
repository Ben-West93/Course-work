Title: Machine Learning Engineer Intern
Company: Fernway Learning
Location: Austin, TX (on site)

Fernway Learning makes reading-practice apps for primary schools. We use machine learning to recommend the next story for each student and to flag when a student may need extra help.

## About the internship

A 6-month paid internship on the recommendations team, with a strong chance of a full-time offer. You'll own one model improvement end to end, from analysis to an A/B test.

## What you'll work on

- Explore student activity data with pandas and Jupyter notebooks
- Train and evaluate models with scikit-learn and PyTorch
- Write clean, tested Python for the feature pipeline
- Present results to the product team every two weeks

## What we're looking for

- Currently studying or recently graduated in computer science, statistics or a related field, or equivalent bootcamp experience
- Solid Python, including pandas and NumPy
- Coursework or projects in machine learning: classification, regression, model evaluation
- Familiarity with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Git basics

## Bonus points

- Hugging Face Transformers
- Experience with recommendation systems
- Interest in education technology

## Pay

$32 per hour, 40 hours a week.
